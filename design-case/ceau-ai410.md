## The four stages

| Stage | What the student does | Role of AI | What is assessed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Create / Scaffold** | Get a working version | Used freely | Working artifact |
| **Evaluate** | Test validity, identify flaws | | Quality of critique |
| **Analyze** | Explain mechanisms and trade-offs | | Depth of explanation |
| **Understand & Refine** | Change and defend the work | **Without AI** | Unaided competence |

## Grading logic: 20 / 80

- Create — 20%
- Evaluate + Analyze + Understand/Refine — 80%
