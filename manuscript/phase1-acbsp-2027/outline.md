# Outline — Beyond Bloom's Pyramid (Phase 1)

> Due: November 9, 2026 (outline + reference library)

**Thesis (provisional):** Generative AI reconfigures how learners create, judge,
explain, and demonstrate knowledge; a sociomaterial lens shows why assessment
must shift weight from the AI-assisted artifact to the learner's evaluation,
analysis, and unaided understanding — operationalized as CEAU.

## 1. Problem
- AI-generated deliverables obscure what students know
- Risk of over-reliance; need to preserve academic rigor
- 

## 2. Theory
- Sociomateriality (Orlikowski 2007; Orlikowski & Scott 2008): practice as
  constitutive entanglement of the social and the material
- Human and material agency in learning practices
- Bloom's Taxonomy (Bloom 1956; Anderson & Krathwohl 2001) as reference point,
  not a hierarchy to be declared obsolete
- 

## 3. Design case — CEAU in AI 410
- Create / Scaffold → Evaluate → Analyze → Understand & Refine
- 20 / 80 grading logic and why
- Active-learning components: writing, reflection, real-world simulation
- Limitations: design case, not impact study
-
