## Which event?

Our notes treated January 19 as the AI Forum deadline. It isn't: January 19
is the deadline for the **ACBSP Conference '27** call. The AI Forum has no
public call yet.

| | ACBSP AI Forum 2027 | ACBSP Conference '27 |
|---|---|---|
| **When / where** | March 11–13, 2027 · San Diego (in person only) | June 24–26, 2027 · Baltimore |
| **Theme** | *From Fragmentation to Framework: Tools and Practices for GenAI in Business Education* | *The AI Revolution: Reshaping Business, Reinventing Education, and Transforming Our World* |
| **Call for presentations** | **Not posted.** Agenda page says speaker announcements and session details "will be shared soon." | **Open** via a Cvent abstract portal |
| **Deadline** | Unknown | **January 19, 2027** (time zone not stated) |
| **What is submitted** | Unknown | Presentation abstract, not a full paper |
| **Session length** | Unknown | 30 minutes, including introductions and Q&A |
| **Registration** | Presenters must register: $700, or $600 by Jan 31, 2027 | $200 off for one author per accepted presentation; all other costs are the presenters' |

## ACBSP Conference '27 — verified requirements

- Submit through the Cvent abstract portal (account required). Submissions
  outside the portal are not accepted.
- Multiple submissions per person are allowed.
- At least one presenter must currently be employed at an ACBSP member
  institution.
- Presentation is 30 minutes including introductions and Q&A.
- If accepted, written confirmation of participation is due **February 26, 2027**.
- Presenters receive a digital certificate.

**Best-fit tracks for this paper**

- *Conference theme → Teaching with AI*: practical strategies and case studies
  for integrating AI into business courses.
- *Accreditation Standards → Student Learning Assessment*, if we lead with the
  assessment-design contribution.

## Implications for the plan

1. **Choose the target event on November 13.** The forum (March) and the
   annual conference (June) are separate events. One option is both: a
   practical session at the forum, if a call opens, and the conference
   abstract by January 19.
2. **Both take presentation proposals, not papers.** This fits a "short form
   now, full article later" route, with the full conceptual paper going to a
   journal.
3. **January 19 stays as the planning target.** It is a verified deadline, but
   for the Conference '27 call, not the AI Forum.
